Field Protocols

Essential Field Guides & Wilderness Ethics

Core principles designed by conservation naturalists and field guides to prepare travelers for respectful, low-impact nature immersion.

Core Standard

Leave No Trace 7 Principles

Universal backcountry stewardship protocols designed to protect soil crusts, fragile alpine flora, and wildlife habitats.

  • Travel & camp on durable surfaces
  • Pack out all trash, waste & food scraps
  • Leave rocks, plants & artifacts untouched
Ethical Protocol

Wildlife Proximity Matrix

Visual distance guidelines to prevent animal stress, flight-response triggers, and dangerous human habituation in the wild.

  • 100+ yards from large carnivores
  • 25+ yards from nesting birds & ungulates
  • Zero flash photography or call-lures
Cultural Respect

Indigenous Land Etiquette

Honoring generational stewardship traditions, customary tenure protocols, and sacred natural sites across ancestral territories.

  • Request permission before taking portraits
  • Respect seasonal taboo & resting zones
  • Support elder-led interpretive walks
Watershed Care

Aquatic & Stream Defense

Safeguarding potable mountain streams, glacial springs, and coral reefs against sunscreen toxins and microplastics.

  • 200-foot campsite setback from water
  • Mineral zinc non-nano sunscreen only
  • UV filtration over disposable bottles
