Visitor Etiquette

Sustainable Travel Practices

Small, conscientious decisions combine to safeguard fragile ecosystems and support local communities for generations.

Respect Wildlife & Habitats

Maintain safe distances, refrain from feeding or chasing fauna, and never touch fragile marine or forest life.

Minimize Waste

Avoid single-use plastics, carry durable water filters, and pack out all wrappers and organic fruit peelings.

Follow Marked Trails

Stay within established paths to prevent alpine soil erosion, vegetative trampling, and trail degradation.

Support Local Businesses

Purchase directly from neighborhood artisans, hire resident guides, and patronize independent village eateries.

Respect Local Traditions

Dress modestly where requested, ask permission before photographing people, and learn basic greeting phrases.

Choose Responsible Transit

Opt for passenger rail, group electric transfers, or walking where practical to diminish expedition carbon output.
